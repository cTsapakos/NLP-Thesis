This thesis explores the use of natural language processing (NLP) techniques for analyzing customer satisfaction, focusing on the comparative evaluation of algorithms through the MATLAB Text Analytics Toolbox. 
Initially, the theoretical framework and functions of the toolbox are presented, followed by the application of techniques such as sentiment analysis, topic modeling, and text classification on data from online reviews.
The study includes a performance comparison of sentiment analysis algorithms between the MATLAB toolbox and GPT-4, as well as an optimization of topic modeling methods, specifically (LDA).
Additionally, the performance of transformer models, such as BERT, is evaluated using standard performance metrics.
The objective is to draw conclusions regarding the effectiveness of different algorithms in analyzing customer satisfaction.
